How to read Model Value

Transfermarkt market values are used only to calibrate the euro scale, never copied. When Model Value sits well above the listed Market Value, the player is flagged as UNDERVALUED. Methodology

How every number on this dashboard is built.

Model Value (€) — metrics in, price out

Model Value is not a copy or rescale of a player's own Transfermarkt market value. It's the output of a single regression fit once across the entire player pool:

ln(Market Value + 1) ~ Composite performance score
   + Age + Age²
   + ln(Minutes played)
   + League Strength (0–100, pyramid position)
   + Position

Market values are used only in aggregate, to teach the model what euro amount the transfer market typically attaches to a given level of output, age, league context and position. Once fit, the coefficients are applied to every player's own metrics — including players with no listed market value — to produce Model Value: what the market should pay for a player with those underlying numbers, independent of what they happen to be priced at today.

Undervalued Ratio = Model Value ÷ Market Value. A ratio well above 1.0 means the player's output/age/league profile is worth more than the market currently prices them at — the definition of undervalued used throughout this dashboard. Players with no market value on file still get a Model Value (useful for lower-league/youth scouting) but no ratio (NO MARKET COMP).

Two performance scores

League-Relative Score
Percentile vs the player's own league/position peers only — reads as "how dominant is he in his current league."
Composite Score
League-Relative Score scaled down by league strength — cross-league comparable, used for Model Value, sorting, and Youth Prospects, so a standout in a weak league isn't confused with a standout in a strong one.

League tiers

Tiers are inherited from a curated football-pyramid table (division position in each country's league system — not derived from market value). Leagues absent from that table get a conservative fallback tier from their division depth so nothing is left unranked.

Peak-age windows

Position-specific windows classify every player as RISING, in their PEAK WINDOW, or PAST PEAK. The flagship "Undervalued & Not Past Peak" board excludes anyone already past their window.

Youth vs. senior

Youth league
The whole competition is youth (Tier 6, or filename-tagged U17/U19/…) — every team in it is Youth.
Youth team
Inside a senior league, reserve/academy sides are flagged by name pattern (…II, …B, U15–U23, Youth, Junior, Academy, Reserves) so first-team recruitment boards aren't diluted by feeder-team stats.

Physical / quick-league fit

Benchmark
Czech First League — a fast, duel-heavy, physically demanding top flight.
Method
Every player's duel/aerial/tempo metrics are z-scored against the Czech First League's own per-position averages (not their own league's). A high score means they already produce Czech-top-flight-level physical/tempo numbers. Goalkeepers are excluded from this board — duel-rate stats are too low-sample and not the relevant style question for them.
